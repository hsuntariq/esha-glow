The house

Ten years of quiet,
unhurried beauty work.

ESHA Glow started in 2015 as a single bridal chair and a very stubborn idea: nobody should share their artist. A decade later we're a full house — hair, skin, nails, spa and a dedicated bridal suite — and that rule hasn't moved. One guest, one artist, one appointment.

Every product on our shelf is professional grade and dermatologist tested. Every tool is sterilised between guests. Our senior team trains twice a year with international educators, because the standards you're paying for should keep rising.
